Menu (Room service & Outside tables)

Shared by both ordering channels. Untick "Room svc" or "Outside" to hide an item from that channel's guest app without deleting it.

Pick "Drinks" as the section to reveal the drink group field — that's what puts a cocktail, wine, beer or soft drink in the right place in the guest app. Picking "Wine" as the drink group reveals Wine type (Sparkling/White/Rosé/Red), used to group the wine list for guests. Use Variants for things guests choose a size for — e.g. Half:3.25, Pint:6.50 or Small:5, Medium:7, Large:9, Bottle:22 — leave it blank for a single-price item. Tick "Cooking pref?" for steaks (in From the Grill or Steak Night) — guests are asked rare/medium rare/medium/medium well/well done, then pick 2 sides, then a sauce. Tick "Steak side?" on any Sides item to make it one of the free choices in a steak's "pick 2 sides" step. Tick "Steak sauce?" on any item to add it to the steak sauce list — both work across every section, so Steak Night can share the same sides/sauces as From the Grill without duplicating them. "Course" only affects Desserts right now — every kitchen printer gets a full copy of the food check, except the desserts printer, which only fires when the order actually has a dessert on it — food items only, hidden for Drinks. New items are available to both Room service and Outside by default — untick either in the table below if an item shouldn't appear on that channel.
