# Team Training: what we train, who, and why

Twenty short sessions (10 to 15 minutes, on the phone, 10 questions, pass mark 8 out of 10) cover the awareness training the law expects every member of the team to have. They sit alongside, and do not replace, the practical and accredited training listed lower down.

This is our reading of the law and good practice, written from public guidance. Ward Hadaway (HR and employment) and your insurer or a health and safety adviser should confirm it for the venue before you rely on it.

## What these sessions do not replace
These need an accredited course, a practical, or a certificate. Log them as certificates in the Training record (person > Add a certificate) so expiry dates are tracked.
- **Food hygiene Level 2 certificate** for everyone who prepares or handles open food; a **Level 3** (supervising food safety) for the head chef and kitchen supervisors.
- **Allergen certificate** (recognised provider) for kitchen and food-serving staff.
- **First aiders**: Emergency First Aid at Work (1 day) or First Aid at Work (3 days), renewed every three years. At least one trained first aider or appointed person on every shift.
- **Fire marshals** (practical course) and a **fire extinguisher practical** for the marshals.
- **Personal licence** (APLH) for the designated premises supervisor, and for any manager who authorises sales of alcohol.
- **Manual handling practical coaching** on the job in housekeeping, the cellar and the kitchen.
- **Marquee, staging and rigging competence** for the events team, and **working at height** practicals where towers or platforms are used.
- **Legionella**: a documented risk assessment and a competent responsible person; staff sessions do not replace it.
- **Gas, electrical and pressure equipment** inspections by competent contractors (PAT, gas safe, pressure systems).
- The **ACT Awareness** e-learning (free, from Protect UK) is a useful addition to the security session.

## Further sessions worth adding later
- Managers: **right to work checks**; **young workers and working time** (under-18s' breaks, hours and night work); **disciplinary and grievance** (already built as the two-hour BHB Training session).
- Office: **display screen equipment** assessments.
- Events: **crowd and capacity management**; **marquee and temporary structures**; **drink spiking and welfare** (including Ask for Angela).
- Everyone: **mental health awareness**; **lone-working check-in** at closing; **induction checklist** (site walk-round, first aiders, fire exits).

## How the record works
- **Issue** a session in Training record > Settings. From that day everyone it is required of has **7 days** to pass it (a new starter has 7 days from their start date). The number of days can be changed per session.
- After a pass, the session **renews** on the pass date plus the renewal months (12 by default, changeable per session). A pass made before a session was re-issued does not count, so **Re-issue** makes everyone do it again (use it when the content changes).
- Who is required: a session marked "Everyone" is required of all staff; area sessions are required of people who work in that area. Set each person's areas in Training record (tap their name). Someone with no areas set is only required to do the "Everyone" sessions.
- **Overdue** means past the due date, or past the renewal date. Filter the record by Overdue, or press **Overdue report** for a printable list grouped by department to hand to the managers.
- An email goes to anyone overdue every morning, at most once a week each, with a copy summary to the admins. The switch is in Settings.
- Every attempt is stored, pass or fail, with the score, session version and time taken.
