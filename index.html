Menu (Room service & Outside tables)

Shared by both ordering channels. Untick "Room svc" or "Outside" to hide an item from that channel's guest app without deleting it.

Pick "Drinks" as the section to reveal the drink group field — that's what puts a cocktail, wine, beer or soft drink in the right place in the guest app. Picking "Wine" as the drink group reveals Wine type (Sparkling/White/Rosé/Red), used to group the wine list for guests. Use Variants for things guests choose a size for — e.g. Half:3.25, Pint:6.50 or Small:5, Medium:7, Large:9, Bottle:22 — leave it blank for a single-price item. Tick "Cooking pref?" for steaks — guests are asked rare/medium rare/medium/medium well/well done and then offered a sauce upsell (any other item in the same section becomes the sauce list). "Kitchen printer" decides which of the three kitchen station printers gets this item (a master copy always prints separately, regardless of this setting) — food items only, hidden for Drinks. New items are available to both Room service and Outside by default — untick either in the table below if an item shouldn't appear on that channel.
