Your Portals

Signed in once, everything below opens without asking again.

Settings — Tiles

Every tile, under the heading it shows beneath on everyone's front page. Change a tile's heading with the box beside it, or make a new heading first. Tiles are always listed A to Z inside a heading. Nothing changes for anyone until you press Save.

Settings — Welcome message

The message people see the first time they sign in. The preview is exactly how it looks. Write {first} in the heading to put the person's first name in. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

Preview

Settings — View as

Pick a person to see the portal the way they see it: their tiles, the tabs inside each, and whether they can only look. This is a preview. It does not sign you in as them, so nothing you do here is recorded under their name.

Settings — PO Tracker

The one place PO Tracker roles, departments, nominal codes and reminder emails are set. The tracker copies them from here every few minutes and whenever someone signs in. Suppliers stay in the tracker itself.

Profiles

A profile is the access a type of team member gets - pick it when adding someone and every tile, tab and edit right is set for them in one go. Their access can still be changed person by person afterwards.
