Your preferred time

is a preferred time, and is not guaranteed. We will do our best to give you the time you have chosen.

Table times are confirmed on or before 25 November. If your chosen time is full, we will allocate you a time to keep our bookings spread across the day.

Are you sitting with another booking?

If you have booked separately from friends or family and want to share a table, you need to link your bookings (it is the "Sit with another booking?" box further down this page). We can only seat you together on the day if the bookings are linked.

If you are only sitting with the people on this booking, just confirm below.

Add a person

Another guest joining you? Choose who to add and pay in full online. Once your payment is confirmed they are added to your booking, and you can choose their meal on this page.

Before you send: drinks for your table

Drinks are 10% cheaper when you pre-order, and they will be waiting for you, so you can enjoy a relaxed start without queuing or waiting for your first drinks.

It only takes a couple of minutes, and you can pay online.
